Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026. This explains what AmpLink collects, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

AmpLink Energy Partners LLC (“AmpLink,” “we” or “us”) runs a structured bidding process for commercial and industrial businesses seeking battery storage and demand response, finds sites for developers, and helps battery companies and other partners sell through channel partner programs. You can reach us through our contact form.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email, phone number, company, state, utility, facility type, peak demand and any message you send through our forms.
  • Bills and related files you upload. An electric bill can include your account number, service address, usage and demand figures and billing amounts. We ask only for what we need to read your demand charges, and you may cover account and payment details before uploading.
  • Utility data you authorize. If you choose utility data access instead of uploading a bill, we receive read-only usage and demand data for the facility through your utility or a utility data service, for as long as your authorization lasts.
  • Basic technical data. Our website host and form service may log your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and run your free bill check.
  • To judge whether a battery or demand response program may fit your site.
  • With your signed service agreement or other written permission, to send your site details to qualified providers in a bidding process and to compare their proposals for you.
  • If you ask us to or have signed with us, to re-screen your site each year.
  • Under our Data terms, to build anonymized, combined benchmarks (see “Data terms” below).
  • To run, secure and improve this website, and to meet legal obligations.

When we share it

  • CSPs, battery manufacturers and developers. We do not share your bill or your contact details with a CSP, battery manufacturer, developer or any other company without your written permission. If you sign our service agreement, we send your site details and, where needed, your bill to the providers invited to bid, and we tell you who they are. We also tell you who pays us and on what terms.
  • Service providers. Companies that help us operate, such as website hosting, file handling, email, scheduling, e-signature and customer-record tools. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and business reasons. When the law requires it, to protect our rights, or in a sale or transfer of our business.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep uploaded bills and utility data for 90 days after we send you our one-page read, unless you ask us to keep them for a bidding process. In that case we keep them until the process ends and for 90 days after. You can ask us to delete your bill and contact details sooner.

Data terms

When you submit a bill or utility data for a free bill check, you agree to these Data terms for the facility. The free check is offered in exchange for them. For a paid feasibility study, they are optional.

  • What you allow. You give AmpLink a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use anonymized figures from your bill or utility data (utility, rate name, demand charge, peak kW, monthly kWh, facility type, size range and state), combine them with figures from other facilities, and share, license or sell the resulting benchmarks and reports to developers, battery manufacturers, researchers and others.
  • What we remove first. Your name, company, address, and account and meter numbers.
  • Your authority. By agreeing, you confirm you are authorized to submit this data and to agree to these terms for the facility.
  • Limits. We publish or sell a figure only when it combines at least five facilities, so no one can be picked out. We do not sell or share your bill, your name, your company or row-level data. Buyers must agree not to try to identify anyone.
  • Utility data. If you share data through a utility or a utility data service, we use it this way only as far as that service’s terms allow.
  • Withdrawing. You can withdraw through our contact form. We stop using your data in future reports within 30 days, but we cannot remove a figure from a report or combined figure that already exists.
  • Keeping the figures. We keep the anonymized figures after your bill file is deleted, unless you withdraw.

How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encrypted connections and limiting access to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please send only what we ask for.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, through our contact form.
  • Opt out of our emails at any time by replying “no thanks” or using the unsubscribe link.
  • Depending on where you live, state law may give you additional rights. We will respond to requests as the law requires.

Business use only

Our services are for businesses and organizations. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Links to other sites

This site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the effective date.

Questions about this policy: use our contact form.

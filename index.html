The sites where peaks pile up.

These are the facilities we look at first, whether we are checking a bill for the owner or sourcing a site for a developer.

Cold storage and food processing
Compressors and defrost cycles overlap, and an outage means spoiled product.
Manufacturing
Presses, ovens and large motors start together and set the monthly peak.
Water and wastewater
Pumps and blowers run hard, and public sites often favor no-capital ownership models.
Hospitals
Steady loads plus imaging and HVAC peaks, with a strong need for resilience.
Schools and campuses
Morning HVAC start-up creates predictable peaks across several meters.
Grocery and big-box retail
Refrigeration and long hours, across many stores that can be screened together.
Hotels
Morning and evening peaks from HVAC, laundry and kitchens.
Logistics and warehouses
Large roofs and lots, dock equipment and a growing EV charging load.

About the bill check

What is a demand charge?

A fee based on the highest amount of power (kW) you draw in a short interval during the billing period. Many commercial and industrial rates include one, and it can be a large part of the bill.

How can a battery help?

It can supply power during your peak, which may lower your measured demand. It may also be enrolled in programs that pay for reducing grid use at certain times. Results depend on your rate, load and program eligibility.

Do I have to pay upfront?

Not necessarily. Some CSPs and battery companies own and operate the battery and share the benefits. Terms vary by provider and site.

Does the bill check cost anything?

No. We may be compensated by the CSP, battery manufacturer or developer we introduce a qualified site to, and we say so up front.

Which programs might apply?

It depends on your state, utility and grid operator. Examples include ISO-NE and PJM demand response, utility battery programs such as ConnectedSolutions in New England, and state incentives. Rules change, so please verify current details.

About AmpLink

What is a CSP?

A curtailment service provider: a company that enrolls sites in demand response and storage programs and pays customers for reducing power use at peak times. Many also offer battery storage.

Do you install batteries?

No. The CSP or battery manufacturer handles engineering, installation, contracts and enrollment. We check sites, make introductions and, for battery companies, run co-marketing and referral work through their channel partner programs.

Which states do you cover?

We work with commercial and industrial sites across the United States. Which programs and providers are available depends on your state and utility.
